Privacy

This QR Code Generator runs entirely in your browser. What you type, including links, text, Wi-Fi names and passwords, is never sent to us or anyone else, and it isn't saved: it's gone when you close the page. PNG and SVG downloads are made on your device. There are no ads, no account and no cookies.

kettleforgestudios.com is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical details such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Everything is generated locally on your device — Wi-Fi passwords and text never leave your browser.
