Privacy Policy

Kernelcade

Kettle Forge Studios · Last updated: November 22, 2026

No tracking

The short version

Kernelcade is developed by Kettle Forge Studios. Your privacy matters to us: Kernelcade does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or advertising identifiers.

1. Files & On-Device Storage

Kernelcade accesses only the folders and files you explicitly select using Android’s Storage Access Framework (such as your game library folder, saves, save states, custom covers, and shader packs). All game files, battery saves, save states, and settings remain stored locally on your device.

2. Local Wi-Fi Multiplayer

When you host or join a 2-player Wi-Fi session, Kernelcade communicates directly with the other device over your local Wi-Fi network (LAN) only. No gameplay or network traffic is sent to Kettle Forge Studios or any external server.

3. Optional Auto Box Art (Off by Default)

If you choose to enable Auto box art in Settings, Kernelcade sends HTTPS requests containing game titles to the libretro-thumbnails collection, hosted on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) and at thumbnails.libretro.com, solely to download cover artwork to your device. When this option is off (the default), Kernelcade makes no external internet requests for artwork.

4. In-App Purchases

Optional paid features are processed directly by Google Play Billing. Kettle Forge Studios does not receive or store your payment card or billing details.

5. Children’s Privacy

Because Kernelcade does not collect personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. This Website

kettleforgestudios.com uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites and does not build a profile of you; we only see totals such as page views, referring sites, countries and device types. The Kernelcade app itself does not use it.

7. Changes & Contact

If we update this policy in the future, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. For questions about this policy or Kernelcade, email support@kettleforgestudios.com.
