Pop-out 2.5D depth

The part you won't find in other players.

Old games draw their worlds as flat layers stacked on top of each other: the sky, the hills, the ground, the characters. Kernelcade pulls those layers apart and gives each one its own depth, so the picture looks like a little diorama behind your screen.

  • Tilt to look around. Tip your device and the layers slide past each other.
  • Set the depth you like. One slider takes it from a gentle pop to deep.
  • A look for each game. Tune one game and Kernelcade remembers it for next time.
  • Or play it flat. Turn 2.5D off and the game looks the way it always did.

Pick a folder and play

No setup maze.

Point Kernelcade at the folder where you keep your game files. It finds them, sorts them by system, and you tap one to play.

  • Save anywhere with save states, next to the game's own saves.
  • Rewind a mistake, or fast-forward the slow parts.
  • Touch buttons or a controller. Choose which button does what.
  • Two players on one device with two controllers.
  • Screen effects you can add yourself, plus color themes for the app.

Play together over Wi-Fi

In testing

Two devices on the same Wi-Fi can play a two-player game together, with no cable and no account.

  • Your half only. In split-screen games, each device can show just its own player's view, filling the screen.
  • Link cable over Wi-Fi for handheld games that were made to connect two systems.

Play goes straight between the two devices. Nothing is sent to us.
