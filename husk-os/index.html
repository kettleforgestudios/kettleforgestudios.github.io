Husk OS

A lightweight, modular operating system project for ESP32-S3 devices.

Husk OS is being developed as free, open-source software: a clean core system with simple controls and an efficient e-paper interface, plus optional free apps (Nibs) that owners can choose to install or build themselves. Every build is made for a specific device and comes with clear compatibility instructions. Community contributions will be welcome once the source is publicly released.

Planned

Nibs — optional add-on apps

Husk OS will ship as a bare core. Small optional programs, called Nibs, are planned so owners can add only the features they want after installing Husk OS. The first planned Nibs:

  • CatEars.nib: uses the board's microphone to learn what your cat's meows mean, such as food, door or attention, and shows its best guess on the screen. It can also play short sounds your cat learns to recognize. It learns one cat at a time rather than claiming a universal cat language, and recordings never leave your device and your own computer.
  • Zombie story.nib: a choose-your-path zombie story, played with the two buttons.
  • Weather.nib: current conditions and forecast on the e-paper screen over Wi-Fi.

Nibs are not available yet. Details will be shared once they have been built and tested on real hardware.
