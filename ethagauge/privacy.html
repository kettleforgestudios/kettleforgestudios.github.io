No tracking

The short version

EthaGauge is made by Kettle Forge Labs, part of Kettle Forge Studios. EthaGauge does not collect, store, sell or share personal information, usage analytics, crash telemetry or advertising identifiers. It works offline, has no ads and has no account or sign-in.

1. What stays on your device

The readings you enter, your results, your saved log and notes, and the name you enter for the audit trail are stored only on your device. They are never sent to Kettle Forge Studios, and we cannot see them. Uninstalling EthaGauge deletes them from your device.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include EthaGauge's saved data in your own Google backup, so your log can come with you to a new phone. That backup is stored in your Google account and handled under Google's privacy policy; Kettle Forge Studios never receives it. You can turn backup on or off in your device's settings.

2. Exporting your log

With the optional unlock, you can export your log as a CSV file. EthaGauge creates the file only when you choose to export, and you choose where it goes, such as a folder on your device or an app you pick. Once it leaves EthaGauge, the privacy terms of wherever you send it apply.

3. In-App Purchase

The optional one-time unlock is processed by Google Play Billing. Kettle Forge Studios does not receive or store your payment card or billing details.

4. No ads, analytics or sign-in

EthaGauge contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting tools and no account system.

5. Children’s Privacy

EthaGauge is a tool for workplace and hobby use. Because it does not collect personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. This Website

kettleforgestudios.com uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies, does not track you across sites and does not build a profile of you; we only see totals such as page views, referring sites, countries and device types. The EthaGauge app itself does not use it.

7. Changes & Contact

If we update this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. For questions about this policy or EthaGauge, email support@kettleforgestudios.com.
