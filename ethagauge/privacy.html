Privacy Policy

EthaGauge

Kettle Forge Labs, part of Kettle Forge Studios · Last updated: November 27, 2026

The short version

No tracking

EthaGauge is made by Kettle Forge Labs, part of Kettle Forge Studios. EthaGauge does not collect, store, sell or share personal information, usage analytics, crash telemetry or advertising identifiers. It works offline, has no ads and has no account or sign-in.

1. What stays on your device

The readings you enter, your results, the initials you put on each day's sheet, your profiles and your settings (including the limits you set under Parameters) are stored only on your device. EthaGauge does not ask for internet access, so it cannot send them anywhere. They are never sent to Kettle Forge Studios, and we cannot see them. Uninstalling EthaGauge deletes them from your device.

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include EthaGauge's saved data in your own Google backup, so your log can come with you to a new phone. That backup is stored in your Google account and handled under Google's privacy policy; Kettle Forge Studios never receives it. You can turn backup on or off in your device's settings.

2. Exports and backup files

You can export your readings as CSV files or PDF day sheets, and save a backup file of your data. EthaGauge creates these files only when you ask it to, and hands them to the app you pick in Android's share menu, such as email, a file manager or cloud storage. Once a file leaves EthaGauge, the privacy terms of wherever you send it apply.

When you restore from a backup, EthaGauge opens only the backup file you choose.

3. In-App Purchases

Any purchases made in EthaGauge are processed by Google Play Billing. Kettle Forge Studios does not receive or store your payment card or billing details.

4. No ads, analytics or sign-in

EthaGauge contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting tools and no account system.

5. Children’s Privacy

EthaGauge is a tool for workplace and hobby use. Because it does not collect personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. This Website

kettleforgestudios.com is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no ads or tracking scripts. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site, under GitHub's own privacy statement. The EthaGauge app itself does not use this website.

7. Changes & Contact

If we update this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. For questions about this policy or EthaGauge, email support@kettleforgestudios.com.
