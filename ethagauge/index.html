EthaGauge

Kettle Forge Labs

Proof and gallons for ethanol plants and distillers. An offline Android calculator built on the public TTB gauging tables, with no ads.

Coming to Google Play In development

What it does

Built for proof checks and gauging, on the plant floor or in the still house.

  • True proof from a hydrometer and thermometer reading, corrected to 60°F, with correction factors for your own instruments.
  • Density input for readings from a density meter.
  • Proofing down and blending, including denaturant blends.
  • Tank gallons and net gallons at 60°F, using your tank's strapping chart.
  • Record keeping, with more details to come.

Offline, no ads, no account

No tracking

EthaGauge works without an internet connection. It has no ads, no analytics and no sign-in, and your readings and log stay on your phone. Read the EthaGauge privacy policy.

How it calculates

Proof math follows the public TTB gauging tables in 27 CFR part 30. Net gallons at 60°F follow API MPMS Chapter 11.3.3. Every result shows the table and the lookup it came from, so you can check it.

EthaGauge is a calculation aid. Official gauges follow your plant's procedures and the rules that apply to you.
